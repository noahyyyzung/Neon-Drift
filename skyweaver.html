SKYWEAVER

Swing the skyline • Stop the crime wave

Quest 3 controls:

Trigger (either hand): shoot a web. Aim at a building to swing; aim at a criminal to yank them in and grab them. Release the trigger to let go — or to throw whoever you're holding.

Grip: web-shot a criminal to wrap them up.   A / X: jump, or web-pop out of a swing.   Right stick up/down: reel in/out.   Left stick: walk / steer in the air.   Right stick left/right: snap turn.

Punch by swinging your fists — faster swings hit harder (swing while you're flying!). Red/amber beams and your wrist map show where the crimes are.

Desktop test mode: WASD move, mouse look, Space jump, left-click web (hold), Q left web, E web-wrap, F punch, R/V reel in/out, Shift run.
